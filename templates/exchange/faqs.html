FAQs

Frequently asked questions

What is EcoSwap?

EcoSwap is a community-driven marketplace where people swap or give away usable items instead of throwing them away.

Is it free to use?

Yes, the platform is designed to make sustainable swapping easy and accessible for everyone at no cost.

How do I request an item?

Open an item listing and click the request option. The owner will review your request and either accept or reject it.

What happens after a request is accepted?

The item is marked as swapped, and both users can coordinate the pickup or meeting plan according to their preferences.

Can I list anything?

You can list items that are clean, usable, and safe to exchange. It is best to avoid broken or unsafe products.
