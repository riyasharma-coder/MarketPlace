How it works

Start swapping in 5 easy steps

1

Create your account

Sign up and set up your profile so other members can see what you are offering and where you are located.

2

List your item

Add a title, category, description, image, and location so people interested in your item can find it quickly.

3

Browse listings

Explore what others are offering and filter by category or location to find items that match your needs.

4

Send or review a request

Interested members can send swap requests. Owners can accept or reject requests based on availability and preference.

5

Complete the swap

Once approved, arrange the meetup or handoff and enjoy the feel-good impact of keeping useful items in circulation.
